# Description:

In this project, I worked with my partner to deliver on the client's business inquiry - to find interesting interactions between 18 monthly economic indicators and their respecive lags and leads (+1/-2 years) to determine which did well for predicting their revenue. The data was massive and spanned multiple decades.

# Analysis methodologies:

I worked on a prescriptive regression with ARIMA errors model (ARIMAX).

# Data

Provided in .xlsx format

# Programming:

I used JMP and R for EDA, and modelled the data in R.

#Process
1) Consult with the client's on their business goals, and data specifications.
2) EDA and Initial Data Cleaning.
3) Research which methodologies best reach this goal.
4) Generate lagged, transformed and dummy variables.
5) Prewhiten the independent variables to the response variable.
6) Model and tune the ARIMAX model.
7) Generate necessary visualizations.
8) Consolidate findings into Report and Presentations.
