# Description
The client was planning a clinical trial in the near future. They had a general idea of what resources were available and developed a PICOT Question of "For those in a Weight Loss Group or at Risk of T2D, does a community educational outreach program improve diabetes management after 8 weekly meetings?" With this, I was to provide recommendations to enable the client to conduct their clinical trial while remaining statistically sound and informed of the insights.

# Methodologies
Throughout this project, I provided the clients with direct support to ensure both that I understood their goals and terminologies, as well as that they understood the limits of their possibly small sample size. Once research was completed and details of the community educational outreach program were solidified, I determined two statistical methods that would answer their question.

# Process
1) Consult the clients on their goals and terminology.
2) Research T2D to gain field knowledge.
3) Determine statistical methods, and the if-needed low-sample-size substitutes.
4) Compile findings in a powerpoint for peer review.
5) Compile clinical trial details, and detailed statistical methods in Design of Experiments report for a non-technical audience.
